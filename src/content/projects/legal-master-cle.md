## Context

Legal Master CLE provides continuing legal education through live webinars and on-demand courses. The client needed one platform for registration, accounts, memberships, purchases, live events, and a growing video library.

At Polaire Labs, I led the architecture and delivery from the first backend services through production handoff.

## My responsibility

I owned the APIs, media workflow, third-party integrations, deployment, and technical client delivery. I also designed the operational paths that let the client publish courses, schedule events, and manage access without engineering support.

## Engineering work

### Accounts and commerce

The backend connects user accounts, course purchases, memberships, event registration, and content permissions. Access rules keep paid and member-only video tied to the correct account state.

### Live and on-demand media

The media workflow handles upload, processing, storage, and playback for recorded courses while supporting live webinar delivery. Monitoring and repeatable deployment steps make the service easier to operate.

### Client operations

Payment, email, and analytics integrations connect the learning experience to the client’s business workflow. Documentation and admin tooling support daily course and member management.

## Result

Legal Master CLE runs as a public production platform for U.S. legal professionals. The project shows my ability to deliver a multi-service product and leave the operating team with a system they can manage.
